Your provider bills you separately

This is the part most easily misread, so plainly: AURA is free, and it does not include any AI usage.

If you connect your own Anthropic or Google account, those companies bill you directly, at their own rates, for whatever you use. We take no cut of it, set no prices, and have no visibility into your account or your bill. We charge you nothing, so there is nothing of ours on your bill either.

Set a spend limit on your provider account before you start. Both Anthropic and Google let you cap monthly spend. It is the only reliable protection against a surprise bill, and we cannot refund a provider’s charges.

Will it always be free?

Everything AURA does on your iPhone is free and always will be. We would rather say that plainly than hedge it.

A paid tier may come later, once AURA has enough people using it to be worth charging for. If it does, it will be for something added on top — not for taking away something you already have. We are not going to build an audience on a free app and then put a wall through the middle of it.

AURA has no adverts, no analytics, no data to sell, and no investor to answer to. That is what keeps the privacy promises real, and it does not change with the price.
