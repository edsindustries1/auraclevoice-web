Everything AURA does on your iPhone is free and always will be. Plus exists for one thing: letting you point AURA at a model you pay for yourself, which is also what makes live web results possible.

The free month

Plus starts with one month free. Nothing is charged until the month is up, and cancelling before then costs you nothing.

To be straight with you about the card. Apple runs the free trial, and Apple requires a payment method on your Apple ID before a trial can start — the same one you already use for any App Store purchase. We never see it. No card details are ever entered into AURA or sent to us, because we do not process payments at all.

Your provider bills you separately

This is the part most easily misread, so plainly: $5 a month goes to us for the app. It does not buy you any AI usage.

Plus lets you connect your own Anthropic or Google account. Those companies bill you directly, at their own rates, for whatever you use. We take no cut of it, set no prices, and have no visibility into your account or your bill.

Set a spend limit on your provider account before you start. Both Anthropic and Google let you cap monthly spend. It is the only reliable protection against a surprise bill, and we cannot refund a provider’s charges.

Billing and cancelling

  • Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription, billed through the App Store.
  • It renews automatically until you cancel, and cancelling takes one tap in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Cancel any time during the free month and you are charged nothing.
  • Your subscription follows your Apple ID, so it works on your other iPhones without signing in to anything.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we have no access to your payment details.

Why charge at all

AURA has no adverts, no analytics, no data to sell, and no investor to answer to. The $5 is the whole business model, and it is what keeps the free tier free and the privacy promises real. If we needed to make money another way, the honest versions of those promises would be the first thing to go.
